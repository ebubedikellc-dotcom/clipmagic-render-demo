Delete your data

Updated 25 September 2026

Stop further publishing

Stop your active clipping projects, then use Disconnect beside each connected platform in your ClipMagic workspace. Disconnect removes its saved connection credentials from ClipMagic. You can also revoke ClipMagic access in that platform’s account settings.

Remove temporary clips

Use the deletion control in Finished clips. Temporary video jobs also expire through scheduled cleanup; the current default is one hour.

Request account data deletion

Email Ebubedikellc@gmail.com with the subject “ClipMagic data deletion”. Identify the email used for your ClipMagic account and which data you want removed. The operator may verify account ownership before acting. Do not send passwords, access tokens or app secrets.

Deleting ClipMagic data does not delete posts already published to a social platform. Remove those posts using that platform’s controls.
