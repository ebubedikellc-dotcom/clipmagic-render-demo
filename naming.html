A better name for every clip.

ClipMagic creates titles and tags from the source caption or description. Teach it your preferred titles and it remembers which words you choose. These appear in the social post; they are not burned into the video.

Checking the naming assistant…

This lightweight system does not watch or listen to videos. It needs a source caption or description. Without one, it uses a neutral name.

Your naming style

Each workflow has its own naming style and examples.

Teach by example

Save up to 200 examples. Approved titles teach it which caption words to favor. It reuses your exact title for a matching description; new descriptions get a title extracted from their own text. Simply generating titles does not teach it whether they are good.


Download a backup after teaching. Free hosting can lose saved examples after a restart or deployment.
