Privacy policy

Updated 25 September 2026

ClipMagic processes videos you provide to create clips and, when you authorize a supported social account and request publishing, deliver content to that account.

Information processed

We process your registration email, password hash where a password is used, session cookies, uploaded media, source and destination links, page names, clip settings, titles, and job status. When you connect a platform, we store account identifiers and access or refresh tokens needed to perform the actions you authorize. Operational logs may include request details and errors.

Use and sharing

Information is used to operate your workspace, process clips, manage account connections, troubleshoot failures and publish requested content. Hosting providers process data to run the service. Your chosen social platform receives the media, title and other publishing information required for an upload. ClipMagic does not sell connected-account data or use it for targeted advertising.

Storage and your controls

Video jobs are temporary: the current default cleanup period is one hour. Download clips you want to keep. Use the clip deletion control to remove temporary files earlier. Workspace records and connection credentials are separate from video cleanup. The current test deployment has temporary storage; restarts can also remove saved workspace data.

Use Disconnect beside a platform to remove its saved connection credentials from ClipMagic. This does not delete a post already delivered to that platform. Use the platform itself to remove published posts. For other stored data, follow our deletion instructions.

YouTube and Google

ClipMagic uses YouTube API Services when you connect YouTube. See the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access through Google account permissions as well as disconnecting within ClipMagic.

Cookies and updates

Session cookies support sign-in. Browser storage retains interface preferences. This notice may be updated as the service changes; the date above identifies this version. Contact the operator about access, correction or deletion of your information.
